## 2. The 10–30 Second Short-Form Video Marketing Engine

Operon is architected to be instantly visually demonstrable. Every high-converting video follows a strict 5-beat rhythm:
```
[0-3s] The Universal Agony (Hook)
[4-8s] The Frustrating Old Way (12 clicks, multiple apps, manual formatting)
[9-12s] Operon Invocation (Alt+Space HUD or mobile Share Sheet)
[13-18s] Instant Deterministic Result (Sub-1ms visual transformation)
[19-25s] Value Proof ("Saved 4 minutes. 100% offline. Zero subscription.")
```

### 5 Verified Video Script Templates:

#### Video 1: "The Telegram/Slack Image Cleaner" (Audience: Creators, Marketers)
- **Beat 1:** Showing a phone with 35 screenshot PNGs taking up 420MB with ugly random timestamps.
- **Beat 2:** Old way: Open Photoshop, resize each, export, delete originals (15 minutes).
- **Beat 3:** Drag all 35 into Operon Quick HUD.
- **Beat 4:** Instant click: `Convert & Compress Images to WebP`.
- **Beat 5:** All 35 files converted to WebP (38MB total) with clean ISO dates in 0.4 seconds.

#### Video 2: "The Messy Link Stripper" (Audience: Everyday Tech Users, Privacy Seekers)
- **Beat 1:** Pasting an Amazon / TikTok / Instagram link that is 6 lines long filled with tracking parameters (`utm_source`, `fbclid`, `igshid`).
- **Beat 2:** Hit `Alt+Space` -> Hit `Enter` (`Strip Tracking URLs`).
- **Beat 3:** Clean, 1-line canonical URL is in clipboard with toast: *"Stripped 6 tracking tags"*.

#### Video 3: "Messy Spreadsheet to Clean Markdown" (Audience: Developers, Notion/Obsidian Users)
- **Beat 1:** Copying an Excel / Google Sheets table.
- **Beat 2:** Pasting it into GitHub or Obsidian results in broken tabbed garbage.
- **Beat 3:** Hit `Alt+Space` -> `TSV to Markdown Table`.
- **Beat 4:** Beautiful GitHub-formatted Markdown table pastes instantly.

#### Video 4: "Natural Language Automation" (Audience: Power Users, AI Enthusiasts)
- **Beat 1:** Typing into Operon HUD: *"Whenever I copy a JSON response, format it with 2 spaces and copy back"*.
- **Beat 2:** Local AI compiler synthesizes the DAG plan in 3 milliseconds.
- **Beat 3:** Shows human plan preview: `clipboard.read -> text.format_json -> clipboard.write`.
- **Beat 4:** Tap Approve: Recipe runs offline forever.
